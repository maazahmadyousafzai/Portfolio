FINAL YEAR PROJECT · 2026

MediCare Hospital Management System

My independently developed BS Computer Science Final Year Project. MediCare provides dedicated Patient, Doctor, and Administrator dashboards for appointment booking and management, doctor approval, digital prescriptions, patient ratings and reviews, email notifications, and administrative monitoring.

The thesis documents a three-tier architecture, a six-table relational database, system design, implementation, and evaluation. Its final testing summary records 47 of 47 test cases passed (100% documented test-case pass rate).

Current repository scope: Payment is a simulated reference-submission workflow requiring administrator verification, not a live payment gateway. The source includes a ZEGOCLOUD video-call workflow with a server-side Token04 endpoint. It requires private ZEGOCLOUD credentials and runtime integration testing; it should not be presented as a production-ready telemedicine service.
